# S08-T04 Lifecycle Adoption Record

**Owner:** S08-T04  
**State:** lane implementation

## Durable resources

- `brainMaintenanceProposals` stores cited maintenance proposals by workspace,
  page, expected revision, lifecycle, route, policy generation, and model/prompt
  pair. Review transitions also record reviewer, attempt, transition reason, and
  idempotency fields so duplicate attempts are fenced without losing audit
  context.
- Proposal citations are manifest keys from the immutable maintenance context
  pack; commit validation never accepts citations outside that pack.

## Lifecycle behavior

Maintenance follows Appendix G's machine:
`gathering -> proposed_noop | proposed_revision`, no-op proposals can become
`accepted_noop`, and revision proposals enter `awaiting_review` unless an admin
has graduated the exact model/prompt pair to Autopilot with a passing evaluation
receipt and reviewed samples. Changed model/prompt pairs, non-admin Autopilot,
stale page/tenant/route/policy/lifecycle generations, lifecycle revocation, and
duplicate attempts raise or downgrade before any revision effect. Human review
can accept, edit-and-publish, or reject exactly one cited proposal effect.

## Compatibility and rollback

The table is additive. Rollback sets maintenance policy to Review-first/Off,
fences pending commits, and preserves proposal rows and exact source capture.
Published Brain revisions remain restorable only through the human page history
flow owned by S02/S03.

## Focused proof

Focused proof covers no-op, new/existing cited revision proposals, citation
membership, stale revision and tenant fences, revision budget, self-confidence,
lifecycle revocation, changed model/prompt downgrade, non-admin Autopilot,
review accept/edit/reject, duplicate attempts, prompt-injection rejection, the
review surface, and prompt-injection evaluation export.
