# S04-T04 Lifecycle Adoption Record

**Owner:** S04-T04  
**State:** lane proof pending integration

## Durable resources

S04-T04 adds immutable Slack channel policy resources:

- `channelRoutingPolicies`: per-channel routing epochs for `direct`, `classify`,
  and `capture_only`, with active-pointer indexing and the post-apply source
  routing state.
- `channelDeliveryPolicies`: per-channel delivery generations for
  `requester_private` and `capture_only`, with Slack Connect channels
  structurally locked to `capture_only` delivery.

Both tables are additive. Policy changes append a new epoch/generation instead
of rewriting prior policy records.

## Lifecycle behavior

The pure policy planner enforces the launch envelope before accepting bulk
changes: 100 active joined channels and 25 Client Brain targets. Admin or owner
role is required. Direct routing requires exactly one authorized Client Brain,
Classify requires a non-empty duplicate-free authorized allowlist, and
Capture-only rejects targets. First policy application opens a bounded
pending-source interval at the policy creation time.

Slack Connect channels may use Direct/Classify ingestion, but requester-private
answer delivery is denied; their delivery policy remains Capture-only.

## Compatibility and rollback

The migration is additive and prospective. Normal rollback is a new policy
epoch/generation that supersedes the active policy pointer; historical policy
rows remain audit evidence. Emergency historical revocation remains owned by
S07.
